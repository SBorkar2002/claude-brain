On the Tetriz marketing feature pages, never put lengthy sub-text below a title or heading — not in the hero lede, section ledes, the "how it works" / deep-dive leads, card descriptions, or anywhere. Every subtext stays short, simple and fast to read (roughly one short sentence, ≤ ~14 words).

**Why:** Sejal repeatedly asks to shorten copy; long multi-clause ledes read as heavy and slow, and a reference page having a long lede does not justify keeping it.

**How to apply:** when writing or porting any title + subtext, compress the subtext to a single quick line; trim any reference lede that runs long. Applies across all feature pages (Why Tetriz, AI Adoption, AI Impact, AI ROI, Session Security). See [[feedback-hero-no-cta-extras]].
