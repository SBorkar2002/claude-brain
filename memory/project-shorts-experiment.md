On 14 Oct 2026 Sejal started an experiment: do very short (7 to 15s) looping videos do well on Instagram and YouTube? The brief: basic, funny, eye-catching, no end cards, the video may loop, AJ or any character, and **no need to follow the old episode pattern** (no term, no explainer).

First draft: `comics/scripts/short-make-it-better.md` (~11s). AJ keeps saying "better", the button ends up back at version one, he calls it perfect, then starts over. It is built entirely from existing graph-engineering plates (s12, s32, s18, s23, s19, s21) plus screens drawn in code, so the only cost is VO and Remotion. The backup is `standup` (needs a new Ken plate).

**Why:** this tests the format, so the plan changes one variable only: a proven character, reused art.

**How to apply:** shorts can break the long-episode rules on pacing (hold 0.3 to 0.5s, not ~1s), but they keep hard cuts, SFX lettering, captions, the AI mark, and AJ's AI register. The open question is whether panel 6 can break the greyscale + teal palette. Related: [[feedback-no-end-cards]], [[feedback-aj-ai-voice]], [[project-comic-cast-lock]].
