Take-home received ~8 Oct 2026, brief at `~/Downloads/Design_Test.pdf`. 4-6 h of work, 3-day window, AI assets allowed but all page design manual in PS/AI. Deliverables in one PDF + layered PSD/AI: 1-page shot plan, 1-2 comic pages (6-10 panels, A4 2480x3508), Kabir Rana hero sheet (5 expressions + 1 action pose), half-page process note. Graded on character consistency (headline), clarity, paneling, compositing craft, one unifying grade, cricket authenticity, lettering. They WILL open the layers.

Sejal's plan: generate on Higgsfield, edit in Photoshop. Direction I gave (8 Oct):
- Generate ingredients (Kabir on plain grey, empty plates, FX), not finished panels; a flat image per panel reads as collage.
- Character sheet first; it is the reference fed into every panel generation. Greybox each panel before generating.
- Spine: the crowd's war drums stop when he walks out and return chanting RA-NA on the last ball. Hand-built scoreboard HUD tracks 14 off 6 down to the win.
- Over: beaten / refuses single ("NO!") / six / four (off-panel) / bouncer to helmet / cover-drive four = 14. Setup 166/7 chasing 180, run out at bowler's end on 19.6 so he takes strike.
- 2 pages, 10 panels; face shown only in P2 and P10, helmet carries identity elsewhere (bookend + consistency saver).
- One neon accent (cyan) on amber/blue-black, not cyan+magenta.
