Sejal rejected the Green Gallery "herbarium editorial" direction (Fraunces serif, DM Mono specimen labels, hairlines, paper) as "very AI generated", same reaction as Instahyre v2 warm-editorial with Instrument Serif. She asked me to stop acting as an assistant and think like a peak-career senior designer/advisor who knows design and dev.

**Why:** the editorial-serif + mono-label + paper + hairline formula is the default "premium" output of AI tools; it reads anonymous, not authored.

**How to apply:** avoid that formula entirely. Give each project a distinct, opinionated register tied to its product meaning (Sourced = night gallery; Green Gallery = cute and bubbly). Take decisions myself with a stated rationale, and gate large builds with a style tile for approval before building 20+ screens. Keep her palette when she approves it.
