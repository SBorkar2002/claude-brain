Launch video (and future videos): after a VO line finishes over a product panel/dashboard, HOLD the panel on screen ~0.8–1s (silent delay) before moving on — panels were being removed too quickly to read. Duration is no longer capped at 2:00; up to 2:30 is fine. NOTE: 1.5–2s holds felt TOO LONG to Sejal ("weird watching them just pause") — ~1s is the sweet spot.

**Why:** Major exec feedback (Aug 2026) — transitions too fast, viewers can't take a good look at the panels (e.g. the org prompt library). Sejal: "we are going to keep repeating this pattern in other scenes as well."

**How to apply:** Split the master VO into playback segments with silent gaps at sentence boundaries (never edit the mp3); extend the scene's SEQ_*, shift post-hold beats; keep subtle ambient motion during the hold (glow breathing, cursor blink) so it doesn't look frozen. Applies to every panel-reveal scene. See [[feedback-continuation-scenes]], [[project-launch-storyboard]].
