- **Don't use "Tetriz Transform" (or any pillar name) as a customer-facing product name in VO or on-screen copy.** "Transform" is internal pitch-deck taxonomy (the Observe/Transform/Govern pillar structure), not a name Tetriz markets externally.
  **Why:** Sumit's direct correction on the v4 Transform script: "There is nothing like 'Tetriz Transform'. The word transformation is only for pitch decks."
  **How to apply:** Category-claim scenes should say "Introducing Tetriz" and lead with the outcome ("AI-native, faster"), not name a pillar. Feature-level names (e.g. "Harness Intelligence", "Weekly AI Coaching") are fine — those are real product features, not the pillar-name problem.

- **Center messaging on "AI-native, faster" as the throughline, not abstract setup/skill contrasts.** A hook built on an indirect metaphor (e.g. "two engineers, same tool, one ships, one doesn't, it's a setup gap") read as confusing to him even though it tested well internally as "punchy."
  **Why:** Sumit's reaction to the hook scene: "Confusing what is this?" He wants the AI-native vs. AI-usage gap stated plainly, fast — not built up through a scenario the viewer has to decode.
  **How to apply:** Open with the literal contrast (everyone uses AI / not everyone is AI-native) before reaching for a more oblique storytelling device. Save the sharper scenario-based lines (like the two-engineers setup gap) for a product-evidence scene, after the premise is already legible.

- **Don't frame coaching/knowledge-sharing features as "taking from top performers and giving to others."** Reads as extraction, not enablement.
  **Why:** Sumit on a "your best engineers know things the rest don't, we pull those patterns out" line: "This makes it feel we are just moving information from best engineers to others. The key here is that we are teaching best practices to everyone."
  **How to apply:** Frame these features around the org-wide outcome (best practices reach everyone) rather than the source (best engineers hold the knowledge). Subject of the sentence should be "everyone gets taught," not "engineer X's knowledge gets extracted."

- **"AI-native" is a substantive, multi-dimensional claim — don't let three stat cards stand in for it without checking with Mohit.**
  **Why:** Sumit on a metrics-only "that's your org, AI-native" closing scene: "It's a lot more than this to be AI native. Let's discuss and get pointers from Mohit here."
  **How to apply:** Before finalizing any scene that claims a metric delta = "AI-native," loop in Mohit on what should actually evidence that claim. Treat metric-only AI-native payoffs as a draft/placeholder until that conversation happens, not a locked scene.

- **Video doc headers should reflect the org-transformation narrative, not generic "product launch" framing**, per Sumit questioning whether "Product Launch Video" was the right label for a video about getting an org to AI-native. See [[project-video-pipeline]] for the broader pipeline this feeds into.
