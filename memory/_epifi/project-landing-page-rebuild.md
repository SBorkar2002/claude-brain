NOTE: numbers are illustrative placeholders; AI Impact sub-page frame NOT yet built (same template, would be next). **Reusable Figma gotcha (IMPORTANT):** `figma.createAutoLayout()` frames default to a solid WHITE `#FFFFFF` fill AND `clipsContent=true`. The white fill shows as a faint rectangle behind text on ANY non-pure-white ground (e.g. the #FAFBFB page ground), and as obvious white blocks on dark panels — so it must be cleared on EVERY layout wrapper, not just dark ones. Best practice: set `.fills=[]` at creation on every wrapper frame. To retrofit, traverse and clear white fills where `cornerRadius===0 && no strokes && no effects` (that leaves real cards/panels/nav/buttons intact). Do NOT use `clipsContent` to distinguish wrapper-vs-card — it's true on both.
